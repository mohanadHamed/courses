# Fundamentals of Computing Systems and Digital Platforms (13411133) — course notes

Course-specific rules only. Global rules are in the root `AUTHORING.md`.

## Timing

- 2 lectures × 60 min (2 credit hours). Lesson A = syllabus Lecture topic, Lesson B = syllabus Seminar topic.
- Size every lesson (HTML, PDF, slides) for **60 minutes**. See the size table in the root `AUTHORING.md`.
  `check_size.py` warns at every render when a lesson or its slides do not fit.
- No class activities. Every lesson ends with a ~10-min Game challenge
  (1–2 "Try it") that each student does alone.

## Lessons learned

- Week 1 (2026-10-17): group work and physical activities are not always
  possible. The pair activity (lesson 01) and the group device survey
  (lesson 02) became solo Game challenges: Rocket Race and Sky Farm.
