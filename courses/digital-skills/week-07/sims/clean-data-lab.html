Clean Data Lab: tidy the sales list with AI advice

Huda copied the sales from three notebooks into Excel. Red cells have a problem. The AI gave six steps; one step is a bad idea. Use only the good ones.

AI: To clean your sales list: 1) Remove the empty rows. 2) Remove the duplicate rows (Data › Remove Duplicates). 3) Remove extra spaces with TRIM. 4) Write each Type the same way (Find and Replace). 5) Make “12 JD” a number. 6) Delete the rows with a Total under 12, they are too small to matter.
