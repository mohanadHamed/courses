# Digital Skills (01101154) — unit plan

Status: approved on 2026-10-28 (`plans/restructure-19-lectures.md`, version 2). Source: the official syllabus (2026-2027, Drive) and the ILO–PLO matrix.
One unit = one week of the syllabus (AUTHORING.md, "Units and sessions"). `schedule.yml` holds the same map for the site and the course package; if they differ, `schedule.yml` wins. "From" names the old lectures and sections that each session is built from.

| Code | ILO (with PLOs) | Bloom | K/S/C |

| A3 | Describe the main features and uses of Microsoft Word, Excel and PowerPoint. (PLO 3) | 2 | K |

| C3 | Identify the basic features of Microsoft PowerPoint used to create presentations. (PLO 2, 3) | 1 | K |
| C4 | Use generative AI tools to draft, edit and improve Word documents, Excel worksheets and PowerPoint presentations. (PLO 2, 3) | 3 | S |

A1, A2, A3, C1, C2 and C3 are the six outcomes of the university description
(same text and Bloom level). B1, C4 and D1 add the use of AI.

- Core: Lectures 1–10 and all 15 online activities. Flexible: Practice 1–5.
- Each online activity practises its unit and never repeats the lecture
  (fixes pattern 7). In units 10 and 12, where the face-to-face session is a
  flexible practice, the online activity carries the core idea.
- AI is the main topic of units 2, 5, 7, 10, 11, 12 and part of 13–15: more
  than the required quarter.
- The AI-use statement starts in unit 11; update `NOTES.md` (today: "from
  lecture 23").
- Old lecture 1: replace the job-advert Lab task with a short AutoSum task.
  Old lecture 3: shrink "how AI works" to two bullets and teach "what AI does
  well", which the syllabus names.
- No homework (the online activities replace it).

## Tasks with AI (ideas)

AI is already the subject of many sessions; the Tasks with AI there are the Office tasks done with AI help at a higher level (a formatted report, a budget with formulas and a chart), always with checking and, from unit 11, the AI-use statement.

## Notes

- **Independent study first.** Pages, slides and online activities never require classmates (the course project has no peer feedback).
- The face-to-face margin: Lectures 1–10 are core; Practice 1–5 are flexible (taught when time allows, studied at home otherwise). All 15 online activities stay: they are asynchronous and not lost to holidays.
- No homework: the online activities replace it. Their model answers are in `courses-private` (`week-WW/online-NN-solutions.md`).
- The AI-use statement starts in unit 11.
