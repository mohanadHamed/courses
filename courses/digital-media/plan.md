# Digital Media Technologies and Creative Production for Games (13411134) — unit plan

Status: plan v3 of 2026-10-31 (`plans/restructure-status.md`), after plan v2 of 2026-10-28 (`plans/restructure-19-lectures.md`). Source: the official syllabus (2026-2027, Drive) and the ILO–PLO matrix.
12 units (core weeks) and 2 practice weeks (filling weeks) (AUTHORING.md, "Units and sessions"). `schedule.yml` holds the same map for the site and the course package; if they differ, `schedule.yml` wins. "From" names the old lectures and sections that each session is built from.

## Course ILOs

| Code | ILO (official text, with PLOs) |
|---|---|
| A1 | Explain the elements of storytelling and screenwriting and their role in the gameplay experience. (PLO 2) |
| A2 | Explain the fundamentals of intellectual property rights for digital content. (PLO 2, 4) |
| B1 | Analyze a game story or script and build a sequential narrative structure for game stages. (PLO 2) |
| B2 | Evaluate the quality of audio and visual content and its suitability for the gameplay experience. (PLO 2, 4) |
| C1 | Design storyboards for game scenes and stages. (PLO 2) |
| C2 | Produce and edit sound, music, and sound effects for a digital work. (PLO 2) |
| C3 | Process video clips (editing and exporting) to produce promotional and cinematic content for a game. (PLO 2, 3) |
| D1 | Work within a production team and respect intellectual property rights in the content produced. (PLO 2, 4) |

  rights and credits); the project in the practice weeks.

## Tasks with AI (ideas)

a branching story with three endings, edited by the student and drawn as a storyboard; a scene drafted with AI and rewritten in screenplay format; a credits list and licence audit of 20 assets, each licence checked at its source. Never AI images, sound or video as the student's media product (unclear rights; it skips the production ILOs); it is a case in the IP sessions.
