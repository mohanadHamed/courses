# Digital Media Technologies and Creative Production for Games (13411134) — course notes

Course-specific rules only. Global rules are in the root `AUTHORING.md`.

## Timing and textbook

- 2 × 60 min (2 credit hours: 1 theory + 1 seminar). Lesson A = syllabus
  Lecture, Lesson B = syllabus Seminar (analysis, critique, production practice).
- Textbook: Schell, *The Art of Game Design: A Book of Lenses*, 3rd ed.
  References: Collins, *Game Sound* (2008); McKee, *Story* (1997).
- PLO 2 (player experience) is behind almost every ILO: always tie a media
  element to its effect on the player.
- Size for **60 minutes** (3–4 sections). Every lesson ends with a ~10-min
  Game challenge that each student does alone.

## Media in lessons

- Sound demos are generated in the browser with Web Audio (no audio files
  from the internet). Start sound only after a tap.
- Example games are invented (Lantern Keeper, Ember Trail); their screens are
  drawn as SVG illustrations. Never use screenshots, music or video of
  commercial games.
- Production weeks (audio 7–10, video 11–12) need free tools that run in the
  lab (Audacity, DaVinci Resolve): check with me before relying on a tool.

## Lessons learned
