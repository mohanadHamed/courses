Tilt Lab

On a phone, press Use the sensors and tilt the phone. On a computer, drag in the pad: the side you drag to goes down. The accelerometer feels gravity, so it shows the tilt. The gyroscope shows how fast the phone turns.

accelerometer y (m/s²)0
accelerometer z (m/s²)9.8
gyroscope: turning (°/s)0
Source: drag pad

Flat on a table: x = 0, y = 0, z = 9.8 (gravity). Drag the pad to tilt.
