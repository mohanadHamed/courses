# Fundamentals of Computing Systems and Digital Platforms (13411133) — unit plan

Status: plan v3 of 2026-10-31 (`plans/restructure-status.md`), after plan v2 of 2026-10-28 (`plans/restructure-19-lectures.md`). Source: the official syllabus (2026-2027, Drive) and the ILO–PLO matrix.
12 units (core weeks) and 2 practice weeks (filling weeks) (AUTHORING.md, "Units and sessions"). `schedule.yml` holds the same map for the site and the course package; if they differ, `schedule.yml` wins. "From" names the old lectures and sections that each session is built from.

## Course ILOs

| Code | ILO (official text, with PLOs) |
|---|---|
| A1 | Describe the components of computers and smartphones and the function of each. (PLO 1) |
| A2 | Explain the functions of operating systems and the concepts of networking and cloud computing. (PLO 1) |
| B1 | Compare mobile platforms (Android and iOS) and gaming platforms in terms of capabilities and limitations. (PLO 1, 3) |
| B2 | Analyze the impact of device and platform specifications on game performance and compatibility. (PLO 1, 4) |
| C1 | Set up testing environments for games using emulators and real devices. (PLO 1, 3) |
| C2 | Describe the requirements for publishing games on app stores and cloud services. (PLO 3, 4) |
| D1 | Use reliable sources for self-learning and follow developments in digital platforms. (PLO 3) |

- Core: Lectures 1–12 and Seminars 1–12. Flexible: Practice 1 and 2.
- The 15-week plan on the e-learning platform: units 1–7, the midterm week,
  units 8–12, and the two practice weeks; the final exam follows in the
  university's exam week. When weeks are lost, the practice weeks go first.
- "Essentials of …" (plan v2) still holds: each lecture keeps the one idea
  of its seminar that an ILO or the exam needs.
- **Cut (plan v3):** the device check as a lecture section (old 6.3; it is a
  problem of Seminar 5), the USB modes (old Example 6.4). Earlier cuts stay
  cut: process states, time-slice sums, virtual memory, block details,
  HAL / ART / XNU, exact store limits, signing keys, IPv6, MAC, the
  four-layer model, nslookup, tracert, egress and price sums, the weighted
  decision matrix.
- **Homework:** 12, one per unit (Homework 5 joins old 5 and 6); none in a
  practice week; the project in the practice weeks.

## Tasks with AI (ideas)

a comparison table of five phones or consoles from an AI draft, three numbers checked on the makers' pages; a test plan for a game on four devices; a store-listing checklist checked against the official store guidelines (D1, reliable sources).

## Notes

- **Independent study first.** Pages, slides and homework never require classmates.
- No ILO of this course asks for team work (D1 is self-learning with reliable sources). The notes of Lecture 7 have the team activity that version 2 of the plan names, with an individual alternative; the presentations of Practice 2 are individual project presentations.
- Reversed earlier decisions (plan, "Open decisions"; cut as planned): cloud gaming and latency (old 2.3, Lag Lab) leave unit 1 and stay, short, in Lecture 12; the texture-memory sums in 1024 are cut (file sizes still count in 1024).
- Homework: one per unit (12); the project in the practice weeks (syllabus week 15).
