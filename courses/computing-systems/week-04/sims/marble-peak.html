Marble Peak

Roll the marble: collect the 3 blue gems, then reach the dark goal. On a phone, press Use the sensors and tilt the phone: the game reads the accelerometer. On a computer, drag in the round pad (the side you drag to goes down) or use the arrow keys.

Level 1 / 3 Gems 0 / 3 accelerometer x 0, y 0
Input: drag pad or arrow keys

Level 1 is the test level of Byte Bay. Tilt to start.
