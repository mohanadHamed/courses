Check an AI answer: claims, sources and fair words.
An AI wrote a short text about breakfast for the class newsletter.

First, find every claim in the text.
A claim can be checked: a number, a name or a study.
An opinion cannot be checked, so you leave it.

Then check each claim in two reliable sources.
Reliable sources are official offices, universities and encyclopedias.
Open other pages about the claim. This is lateral reading.
Two sources say the café sells about 120 sandwiches, not 200.

Check a study in three ways.
Search its title, check the authors, and open the link.
If all three fail, the source is made up.

Now decide for each claim.
Keep it, correct it with the source, or remove it.

Then read the text again for fair words.
Change a stereotype into neutral words.
Give both sides of a question, with reasons.

Keep a check log: claim, source, result and action.
At the end, add an AI-use statement.
Now practise in Claim Finder and Claim Court.
