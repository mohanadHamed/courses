## Weeks: 11 units and 1 practice week

Units 1 and 2 were taught before v3 and are not changed. The teacher chose
(2026-11-14) to use all nine teaching weeks left for units, with no
separate project week: the course project (D1, mandatory) is built in the
online activities of Units 9–11 and handed in, with its three slides and
its AI-use statement, in Unit 11. Two pairs of rows are merged (4–5 and
12–13); Unit 11 also covers row 15.

Calendar of fall 2026-2027: Unit 3 in platform week 6 (15/11/2026) and one
unit a week to Unit 11 in week 14 (10–14/1/2027). Practice 1 and Online
activity 7 are for self-study before the midterm.

AI (syllabus v4, B2): every unit from 4 on uses an AI assistant for one
step, always with a prompt ready to copy and a check of the result; the
AI-use statement starts in Online activity 4 and ends in the project.
